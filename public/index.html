Curiosity, with headphones.

This prototype explores how differently processed versions of the same music feel. It is a design and interaction test, not an active research study.

The built-in loop is synthesized for this demo. Versions are RMS-matched, not LUFS-validated research stimuli. A/B/C assignments are randomized for each new session.

Completion celebrates participation. It does not measure hearing ability or assign a personality type.
